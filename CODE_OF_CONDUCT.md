# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss evidence and ideas without harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing private information. Make participation welcoming regardless of experience or background. Respect consent when testing networks and handling diagnostic data.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Apply these standards consistently and handle reports confidentially. A private reporting contact must be configured before inviting a public community; this local engineering repository has no community moderation channel yet.
